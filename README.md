## 🚀 About Me

I am a results-driven **Data Analyst / Business Analyst with 2.5+ years of professional experience** in **Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Reporting Automation**. I specialize in bridging the gap between raw data and strategic decision-making.

- 📊 **BI & Reporting:** Designing interactive **Power BI dashboards**, automated **MIS reports**, and executive KPI trackers.
- 🗄️ **Database Querying:** Writing optimized **SQL** queries utilizing complex joins, CTEs, subqueries, and window functions for data extraction and reconciliation.
- 🐍 **Data Science & Python:** Leveraging **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Seaborn)** for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical modeling, and predictive analytics.
- 🔍 **Data Integrity:** Proven track record in end-to-end **data cleaning, validation, quality checks, and variance analysis**.
- 📋 **Spreadsheet Automation:** Streamlining workflows using **Advanced Excel** (Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, VBA Macros) and **Power Automate**.
- 🤝 **Business Partnering:** Collaborating directly with stakeholders for requirement gathering, process optimization, and business storytelling.
- ☁️ **Cloud & Modern AI:** Working with **AWS analytics services** and integrating **Generative AI / LLMs** to accelerate analytical workflows.
